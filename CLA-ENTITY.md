# Radial Timeline — Entity Contributor License Agreement

**Version 1.0**

Use this form when Contributions are made by employees, contractors, or agents on
behalf of a company, institution, or other legal entity — that is, whenever an
employer or client may hold rights in the work under an employment agreement,
work-for-hire rule, or assignment.

Individuals contributing on their own behalf should use `CLA.md` instead.

---

## 1. The agreement

The Entity identified below agrees to **every term of the Radial Timeline
Individual Contributor License Agreement, Version 1.0** (`CLA.md`), which is
incorporated here in full, with these substitutions:

- "You" means the Entity.
- "Contribution" includes any work Submitted by a Designated Contributor listed
  below, whether or not it was created within the scope of their employment.
- The representations in Section 6 of `CLA.md` are made by the Entity as to all
  Contributions Submitted by its Designated Contributors.

The Entity represents that the person signing below is authorized to bind it, and
that the Entity holds or controls the rights necessary to make the assignment and
grants in Sections 2 and 5 of `CLA.md`.

## 2. Scope

This agreement covers Contributions Submitted by the Designated Contributors
listed in Schedule A, from the effective date onward. The Entity may add
contributors by sending an updated Schedule A to the address in `LICENSE`;
additions take effect when acknowledged in writing by the Owner.

Removing a person from Schedule A ends their authority to Submit on the Entity's
behalf going forward. It does **not** withdraw, revoke, or affect any rights in
Contributions already Submitted, which are irrevocable.

## 3. Employees acting outside this agreement

If someone Submits a Contribution that the Entity believes it has rights in, but
who is not listed in Schedule A, the Entity agrees to either add them to Schedule
A or notify the Owner promptly at the address in `LICENSE` so the Contribution
can be removed.
