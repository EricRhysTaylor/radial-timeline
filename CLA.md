## Why this exists

Without an agreement, whoever writes a line of code owns the copyright in it.
A project that merges outside code without a signed agreement no longer has a
single owner who can license, relicense, defend, or transfer the whole work — and
that cannot be repaired later without tracking down every past contributor and
obtaining their agreement, one by one.

This agreement is deliberately short and says plainly what it takes. It does not
ask you to give up the right to use your own work.

## How to sign

Comment once on your first pull request with exactly this line, substituting your
details:

```
I have read the Radial Timeline CLA v1.0 and I agree to it.
Name: <your full legal name>
GitHub: @<your username>
Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
```

Your GitHub account authenticates the signature. One signature covers all your
future Contributions under this version of the agreement. If the agreement is
revised, you will be asked to sign again before your next Contribution is merged.

Signatures are recorded in `CONTRIBUTORS.md`. A pull request cannot be merged
before its author has signed.

**Contributing on behalf of an employer or company?** Use `CLA-ENTITY.md`.
