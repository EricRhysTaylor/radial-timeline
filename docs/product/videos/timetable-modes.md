# Video script: Ask the Timetable questions in every mode (Short)

**Kind:** a Short, not a how-to: it explores what the Timetable's filters, groups and sorts mean in each timeline mode. The plugin doesn't link it; the wiki's Timetable page can (thumbnail at "Follow the timeline's mode").
**Length:** about 2:25.
**Frame:** record 16:9 in UHD, then reframe to 9:16, as for the Beats Update Short. Each shot names where the vertical frame sits: the wheel, the table, or a split with the wheel above the table.
**Production files:** `Radial Timeline LLC/Video/Shorts/Timetable Modes/`.
**Written against:** main at 8.0.x (wiki Timetable, "Follow the timeline's mode"; `src/timetable/TimetableView.ts`). Labels in quotes are exact; Bases' own menus are Obsidian's and may read differently in your Obsidian version.

## What the Short says

- **The four modes are the Timetable's ways to read the book.** Narrative lists the manuscript in order. Chronologue lists dated scenes by when they happen. Progress shows how far the work has come. Gossamer brings the beats and their scores forward.
- **The Timetable follows the timeline's mode, or you pin it to another.** A mode changes what the table shows, not only its order: Chronologue's day dividers, Progress's totals on each heading and its overdue dates, Gossamer's beats with their scores.
- **Filters, groups and sorts are questions you ask inside a mode,** not modes of their own.
- **Three things to watch:**
  - A sort set in Bases outranks the mode's order. In Chronologue the day dividers disappear, which is the visible sign, and the drag grips wait until the sort is gone.
  - In Chronologue, rows form one list in story time, so act headings and any other grouping step aside.
  - Outline always reads in narrative order, whatever the mode, because it's for working through the manuscript.

## Before recording

- **Vault:** a throwaway copy of the Odyssey Story Vault, with The Odyssey as the active book. It's told out of order, which is what the Chronologue shot needs: the wanderings Ulysses recounts in Books 9–12 move to the start in story time.
- **Starting state:** the Timetable beside the wheel, in the Radial Timetable view and the Table layout. It follows the timeline (no lock on "Mode"), with no Bases sort and no filter of its own.
- **Progress data:** the Story Vault is a finished book, so give the copy some unfinished work. Pick a dozen scenes with the row checkboxes and use the bar under the table to set some to Todo and some to Working. Give one of them a Due in the past.
- **Check first:**
  - With the timeline colored by Character, picking Ulysses and Telemachus reads "Both present", and their first shared scene is in Book 16, at Eumaeus's hut. The character is Ulysses, Butler's name; Odysseus is an alias in his note.
  - A "This view" filter of Character containing Ulysses keeps his scenes, since Character is a list of links.
  - Grouped by Status in Progress, each Status heading carries Progress's counts (words, overdue scenes).
  - Whether the Odyssey's beat notes carry a Subplot. If they do, shot 9 can filter the beats to one subplot instead. If they don't, a Subplot filter hides every beat, so keep shot 9 as written.
- **Pace:** 1.5 to 2 seconds per click or choice, menus open long enough to read, never sped up.
- **Recorder:** as for the Beat Manager: the shared theme, click highlights, nothing private on screen.

## Shot list

### Opening (0:00–0:20)

**1. Four ways to read (0:00–0:14)**
- Screen: the wheel in Narrative with the Timetable beside it. Switch the timeline's mode to Chronologue, then Progress, then Gossamer. The table changes with each: act headings, then day dividers, then the counts on each heading, then beats with scores.
- 9:16: split, the wheel above the table.
- Caption: Four modes, four ways to read the book
- Voice: "The Timetable follows your timeline's mode. Narrative reads the book in manuscript order, Chronologue in story time, Progress by how far the work has come, and Gossamer by its beats."

**2. Questions, not modes (0:14–0:20)**
- Screen: the Bases toolbar above the Timetable: its filter, sort and group controls.
- 9:16: the table's toolbar.
- Caption: Filters, groups and sorts: questions inside a mode
- Voice: "Filters, groups and sorts aren't extra modes. They're questions you ask inside one."

### Follow or pin (0:20–0:34)

**3. Pin the table to another mode (0:20–0:34)**
- Screen: the timeline in Narrative. In the Timetable's toolbar, open "Mode" and choose Chronologue; a lock appears. The wheel keeps the poem's order while the table lists story time. Then "Mode" → "Follow the timeline".
- 9:16: split.
- Caption: Pin the table to a different mode
- Voice: "Or pin the table to another mode. Here the wheel reads the poem as Homer tells it, while the table lists what happened when. Follow the timeline lets it go."

### Narrative: who shares the page? (0:34–0:58)

**4. Two characters together (0:34–0:58)**
- Screen: Narrative. Right-click the Character column header and color the timeline by it. In the key above the timeline, click Ulysses, then Telemachus; the key reads "Both present". The wheel narrows to the scenes they share and the table fades the rest. Close with "Stop coloring".
- 9:16: the wheel, then the faded table.
- Caption: Narrative · where do they meet?
- Voice: "In Narrative, ask who shares the page. Color by character, pick Ulysses and Telemachus, and the wheel narrows to the scenes they share. Father and son don't meet until Book 16."

### Chronologue: one life in story time (0:58–1:22)

**5. Follow a character through time (0:58–1:22)**
- Screen: switch the timeline to Chronologue. The table becomes one list in story order, with a divider for each day and the beats left out. In the Bases filter menu, under "This view", add Character contains Ulysses. The lens turns on by itself: his scenes stay lit on the wheel, the rest recedes, and a pill names the view. Scroll the table's first rows: Troy and the Cicons, years before scene 1.
- 9:16: the wheel, then the table's first rows.
- Caption: Chronologue · follow one character through time
- Voice: "In Chronologue, follow one life through story time. Act headings give way to days. Filter to Ulysses, and the lens lights his path: the wanderings he recounts in Books 9 to 12 come first, years before the poem begins."

### Watch the sort (1:22–1:40)

**6. A sort outranks the mode (1:22–1:40)**
- Screen: still in Chronologue with the Ulysses filter. In the Bases sort menu, sort by Words. The order changes and the day dividers disappear. Try dragging a row by its grip: it explains what to change instead of moving. Remove the sort; story order and the dividers return.
- 9:16: the table.
- Caption: A Bases sort overrides the mode's order
- Voice: "One thing to watch: a sort you set in Bases outranks the mode. The day dividers disappear — that's your sign — and the grips that move scenes wait. Remove the sort, and story time comes back."

### Progress: what's left? (1:40–1:58)

**7. Unfinished work, by status (1:40–1:58)**
- Screen: remove the Ulysses filter (cut). Switch the timeline to Progress. Filter "This view" to Status is not Complete, then group by Status. Headings for Todo and Working each count their words and overdue scenes, and the late Due cell stands out in red.
- 9:16: the table.
- Caption: Progress · what's left to write
- Voice: "In Progress, ask what's left. Filter out finished scenes and group by status. Each heading counts its words and what's overdue."

### Gossamer: where does the structure slip? (1:58–2:14)

**8. Clear the question (cut)**
- Screen: remove the filter and the grouping, off camera or as a quick cut.

**9. Beats outside their range (1:58–2:14)**
- Screen: switch the timeline to Gossamer. The beats come forward with their latest score for the selected signal, its change since the previous run, and the expected range, with a flag where a score falls outside it; the other rows recede. Hold Shift over a flagged beat's justification to read it in full.
- 9:16: the table, then the wheel's beats.
- Caption: Gossamer · where does the structure slip?
- Voice: "In Gossamer, the beats come forward with their scores. Ask where the structure slips: a flag marks any beat outside its expected range. Hold Shift to read why."

### Outline (2:14–2:20)

**10. Always in reading order (2:14–2:20)**
- Screen: still in Gossamer, switch the Radial Timetable's layout to "Outline": the book act by act, in reading order.
- 9:16: the table.
- Caption: Outline always reads in narrative order
- Voice: "Outline always reads in narrative order, whatever the mode, because it's for working through the manuscript."

### End card (2:20–2:26)

**11. Save the question**
- Screen: the Radial Timeline mark, "Look up: Timetable in the Radial Timeline wiki", and radialtimeline.com. It holds to the last frame with the address on screen for at least three seconds; no fade.
- Voice: "Save a question as its own view, and it's a lens you can reopen. Look up Timetable in the Radial Timeline wiki."
