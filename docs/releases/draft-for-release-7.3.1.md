## Radial Timeline 7.3.1

### Community

- **Daily words count every mode.** Your Community page's daily words now include revising, editing, and planning sessions, not only drafting. The first sync after updating sends your last 12 weeks once so the season catches up, and a vault connected to a different Community profile later sends that profile its own 12 weeks. The Community Share preview lists the new per-day words before anything leaves the vault.
