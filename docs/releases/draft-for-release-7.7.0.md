## Radial Timeline 7.7.0

Runtime moves to Narrative mode, where it follows the order your reader or listener meets the book.

### Runtime view in Narrative mode

- **Runtime follows reading order.** Click **RT** at the top left of the timeline, or the **Runtime** chip beside **Narrative**, to open the Runtime view. Every scene keeps its equal slice; a blue arc inside each slice grows with that scene's runtime, measured against the longest scene, with its runtime at the tip.
- **Running time around the ring.** Each perimeter tick shows the runtime when that scene begins, from `0:00` at the first scene to the book's total at the end of the last.
- **Runtime between two scenes.** Click any two scenes to see the runtime from the first through the second, both included, in manuscript order.
- **Scenes waiting for an estimate stand out.** A scene without a `Runtime` value shows a short red stub; run **Runtime estimator** to fill it in.
- **The Timetable keeps pace.** With the Runtime view on, an open Timetable adds a **Running time** column in manuscript order.
- **Chronologue stays about story time.** Chronologue keeps its Shift and Alt views for elapsed time and planetary calendars, with duration arcs for story time.
