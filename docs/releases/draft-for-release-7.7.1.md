## Radial Timeline 7.7.1

### Timeline

- **Character lanes in Narrative mode.** Turn on **Settings → Core → Point of view → Character lanes** to follow your main characters through the book. Up to four thin lanes sit inside the outer ring, one for each of the characters who are POV in the most scenes. A tall mark is a scene told from their point of view; a short mark is a scene they're in. Gaps show how long a character is gone. Hover a mark to light its scene.

- **Scene titles read right side up all the way round.** Titles across the bottom half of the wheel now run left to right instead of upside down, in every ring and while you hover a scene to show its full title.
- **The rotate toggle is gone.** The arrow beside the outer ring turned the wheel so the lower titles could be read. With every title upright, it had no job left, so the wheel now always opens with Act 1 at 12 o'clock.
