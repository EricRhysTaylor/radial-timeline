## Radial Timeline 8.0.1

New Story Vault editions for Radial Timeline 8.0.

### Story Vaults

- **The Story Vaults use Narration.** Pride & Prejudice, The Odyssey and Sherlock Holmes are repackaged with the 8.0 scene field, so a newly added Story Vault opens without the field-rename alert in Settings.
- **Every book has a Timetable and a subplot map.** Each Story Vault includes a Timetable for each of its books and a Canvas map of its subplots with scene cards.
- **Pride & Prejudice adds pictures of its nine places**, shown with the 29 character portraits in Timetable Panels. Its download is now 20 MB.
- **An earlier Story Vault is offered the new edition.** When you open a vault from an earlier download, the Welcome screen offers the 2026-11-10 edition as a separate download.

### Changes

- **No Pro labels.** Settings, Inquiry, manuscript options and the runtime dialogs no longer mark features as Pro.
