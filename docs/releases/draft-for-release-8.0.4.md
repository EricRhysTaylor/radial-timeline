## Radial Timeline 8.0.4

Scene breaks that hold dialogue reach your exported manuscript, and story maps keep their details while Canvas files sync.

### Fixes

- **Manuscript export:** a scene break written as a `---` block keeps every line inside it in your PDF, Word and Markdown exports, including dialogue such as `Kade: Get down!`. Only the frontmatter at the very top of each scene and matter note is left out of the manuscript.
- **Story maps:** a story map keeps its review details while its Canvas file is still arriving from another device through sync, so **Map out of date — review updates** and text drops work as soon as the Canvas lands. Deleting a map's Canvas still removes its stored details.
