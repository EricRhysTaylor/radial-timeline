**Advanced workflows for authors who want more.**

Radial Timeline is free. Every workflow described on this page ships in the plugin at no cost, and nothing you use today will be taken away.

Pro is a service, not a set of unlocked features. Everything the plugin does stays free. Pro covers hosted analysis and serving readers at scale. Hosted Inquiry runs manuscript analysis on our models and prompts, so you never have to bring an API key.

---

## Pro mode is a switch, not a purchase

**Pro mode** lives in **Settings → PRO** and is on by default. It gathers Radial Timeline's heavier, more specialized workflows under a single toggle, so an author who wants a quieter workspace can switch them off and work in the Core surface instead.

There is nothing to buy here and nothing to enter. Turn Pro mode off, and the workflows below step out of the interface. Turn it back on, and they return exactly as they were.

### Workflows grouped under Pro mode

*   **Inquiry+** — the extended question set and a higher custom-question ceiling per zone. See [Settings → Inquiry](Settings-Inquiry) and [Inquiry](Inquiry).
*   **Runtime estimation** — screen time, audiobook duration, and manuscript-length analysis. See [Runtime estimator](Commands#runtime-estimator), [Chronologue Runtime](Chronologue-Mode#runtime-mode-pro), and [Settings → Core](Settings-Core#runtime-estimation).
*   **Advanced publishing layouts** — the Professional, Signature, Screenplay, and Podcast Script templates, plus designed-style editing, template import, and custom Pandoc/LaTeX metadata. See [Publishing](Publishing) and [Manuscript Export](Manuscript-Export).
*   **Campaign Manager / Teaser Reveal** — multiple APR campaigns and staged public reveals. See [Author Progress Report](Author-Progress-Report).
*   **Saga timeline scope** — view an entire multi-book saga on one timeline.
*   **Custom scene colors and patterns** — author-defined palettes and working-pattern motifs. See [Settings → Core](Settings-Core).

Pro workflows appear throughout Radial Timeline in magenta.

## Where money comes in

Radial Timeline LLC charges for things that cost money to run — never for plugin features:

*   **Hosted Inquiry** — manuscript analysis on our models and prompts, so you never have to bring an API key. Running Inquiry against your own provider key stays in the plugin and stays free: your key, your models.
*   **Serving readers at scale** — hosting a shared timeline and delivering it to an audience.
*   **AI processing in the review pipeline** — analysis that runs on our infrastructure rather than yours.
*   **The web workspace** — the browser-based writing and timeline surface, in development.
*   **Supporter patronage** — optional, and only ever cosmetics or early access to new work. Never features.

These will be available as paid services. They add hosted capacity alongside the plugin; they do not move anything out of it.
