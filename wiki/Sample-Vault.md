A free, fully analyzed working vault built around Jane Austen's *Pride & Prejudice* — the complete novel broken into 61 scene notes with full frontmatter, Save the Cat beat notes, character notes, and a real AI analysis pass already baked in. It's the fastest way to see what Radial Timeline produces when pointed at a full-length novel, with no API key and no setup beyond installing the plugin.

## How to get it

The sample vault is delivered by email:

1.  Click **Get the sample vault** on the plugin's Welcome screen, or visit [radialtimeline.com](https://radialtimeline.com) — both lead to the email signup.
2.  Sign up with your email address.
3.  The download link arrives in your inbox.

## How to install it

1.  Download the `.zip` from the link in your email and unzip it. You'll get a folder named **Obsidian Vault Pride & Prejudice Demo**.
2.  In Obsidian, choose **Open another vault → Open folder as vault** and select that folder.
3.  When Obsidian asks, enable community plugins for the vault, then install **Radial Timeline** from **Settings → Community plugins → Browse**.
4.  The **Welcome screen** opens with the plugin — it detects the packaged book and shows **Open the sample vault**. Click it to drop straight into the finished story on the timeline. (If you skip the Welcome screen, point the plugin at the book yourself under **Settings → Core → Books**, then open the view with **Radial timeline: Open**.)

## What's inside

*   **`Pride & Prejudice/`** — the manuscript as 61 scene notes plus Save the Cat beat notes. Every scene carries full frontmatter (`Act`, `When`, `Duration`, `Synopsis`, `Subplot`, `Character`, `POV`, `Status`, `Publish Stage`, and more) and a complete **Pulse Triplet** analysis with per-beat justifications and model provenance.
*   **`Characters/`** — notes for the full cast, linked from every scene they appear in.
*   **`Source Manuscript/`** — the underlying 1894 George Allen edition of the novel (public domain) and a plain-text synopsis.
*   **`Radial Timeline/Recover/`** — preserved snapshots of past Gossamer runs and Inquiry sessions: the persistent history of how the analysis evolved.

## Exploring it

*   Switch modes with `1` (Progress), `2` (Narrative), `3` (Chronologue), `4` (Gossamer) and hover any scene for its synopsis and Pulse analysis.
*   Try Chronologue's **Shift** sub-mode to see elapsed time between scenes across the novel's calendar.
*   Open the [Inquiry view](Inquiry) to read saved briefings from prior Inquiry sessions. Saved results are browsable with AI enabled. Configure a cloud provider or validated Local LLM to run new analysis.

You're welcome to fork it, edit it, write in it, or strip it for parts — the source text and all included analysis are free to read, copy, and reuse.
