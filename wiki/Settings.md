Access Radial Timeline settings in Obsidian via **Settings → Community Plugins → Radial Timeline**.

The tab strip uses short labels: **Cor**, **Soc**, **Com**, **Inq**, **Pub**, **AI**, **Adv**, and **Res**. This wiki uses the full names, so **Settings → Core** means the **Cor** tab.

Obsidian's settings search finds Radial Timeline settings too. Search for a section such as Scene properties, Story beats, PDF fonts or an API key, and the result opens Radial Timeline's settings at that section, opening it if it was closed.

## Settings Tabs

*   [Core](Settings-Core) — Books, progress and status, runtime estimation, story beats, scene properties, chronology, narration, planetary time, and ring colors.
*   [Social](Author-Progress-Report) — Author Progress Report (APR), progress tracking, styling, publishing automation, teaser reveal, and campaigns.
*   [Community](Settings-Community) — The website connection, then two rows: **Vault → Community** (sharing levels 1 to 4, sharing details, your book's timeline and the scenes asked for on Community) and **Community → Vault** (books from Community to create in this vault).
*   [Inquiry](Settings-Inquiry) — Briefings, action notes, session history, sources, prompts, and corpus thresholds.
*   [Publish](Publishing) — Pandoc, PDF styles, bundled layouts, chapter markers, Parts, and manuscript export workflow.
*   [AI](Settings-AI) — Provider setup, model selection, local/OpenAI-compatible settings, and analysis behavior.
*   [Advanced](Settings-Advanced) — Timeline display options, remap frontmatter field keys, ripple rename, logs, and export-path readouts.
*   [Resources](Resources) — Story Vaults with their editions, then **Learn more**: Getting started, the Radial Timeline Wiki, and YouTube videos.
